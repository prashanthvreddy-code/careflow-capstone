# Pre-Authorisation Requirement Matrix

MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS  
OPERATIONS POLICY MANUAL  
POLICY REF: MHP-OP-2024-PA4  
SUBJECT: PRE-AUTHORISATION REQUIREMENTS AND ESCALATION PROTOCOLS  
EFFECTIVE DATE: 1 JANUARY 2024  

1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE  
1.1 This policy establishes mandatory pre-authorisation requirements, clinical documentation thresholds, approval processing timelines, and appeal routes across all fourteen Meridian Health Partners sites for patients enrolled in MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE, and CIVIC-BASE insurance plans.  
1.2 Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all clinical, scheduling, and billing personnel.  

2.0 GENERAL RULES BY SERVICE TYPE  
2.1 Consultations (CARD-CONS, ORTH-CONS, DERM-CONS, ENDO-CONS, GAST-CONS, PULM-CONS, NEUR-CONS, ENT-CONS) never require pre-authorisation under any plan.  
2.2 Laboratory work and diagnostic testing classified as lab tests (DERM-BIOP, ENDO-PANL, PULM-PFT, ENT-AUDI) never require pre-authorisation under any plan.  
2.3 Imaging services require pre-authorisation based on plan tier as follows: MERIDIAN-GOLD requires no pre-authorisation; MERIDIAN-SILVER requires pre-authorisation with a 3 business day approval window; MERIDIAN-BRONZE requires pre-authorisation with a 5 business day approval window; CIVIC-BASE requires pre-authorisation with a 5 business day approval window.  
2.4 Procedures require pre-authorisation under every plan with the following approval windows: MERIDIAN-GOLD requires 2 business days; MERIDIAN-SILVER requires 3 business days; MERIDIAN-BRONZE requires 5 business days; CIVIC-BASE requires 7 business days.  
2.5 Coverage exclusions: CARD-CATH and ORTH-ARTH are not covered at all under the CIVIC-BASE plan.  

3.0 CARDIOVASCULAR SERVICES  
3.1 CARD-CONS (Cardiology Consultation, list price $220): Pre-authorisation is not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE, or CIVIC-BASE. Clinical documentation needed: standard outpatient referral note. Appeal route if denied: Medical Director Review via Dr. Aris Vance.  
3.2 CARD-ECHO (Echocardiogram, list price $950): Pre-authorisation is not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD; required under MERIDIAN-SILVER with a 3 business day approval window; required under MERIDIAN-BRONZE with a 5 business day approval window; required under CIVIC-BASE with a 5 business day approval window. Clinical documentation needed: cardiologist referral and recent electrocardiogram showing structural or arrhythmic indication. Appeal route if denied: Independent External Review Panel (IERP).  
3.3 CARD-CATH (Cardiac Catheterisation, list price $7,800): Pre-authorisation is required under MERIDIAN-GOLD (2 business days), MERIDIAN-SILVER (3 business days), and MERIDIAN-BRONZE (5 business days); not covered under CIVIC-BASE. Clinical documentation needed: recent abnormal echocardiogram, treadmill stress test report, and attending cardiologist attestation. Appeal route if denied: Meridian Utilization Review Committee (URC) Level 2 Appeal.

11.0 GOVERNANCE AND ENFORCEMENT  
11.1 Any service rendered without obtaining prior approval within the specified business day approval window shall result in an administrative denial and write-off absorbed by the executing Meridian Health Partners clinic site.  
11.2 Escalations regarding denied authorisations must be submitted in writing to Claims Operations Manager Sarah Jenkins within 10 business days of the adverse determination notice.
